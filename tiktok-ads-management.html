Why it works

TikTok works differently from other ad platforms. The feed is full-screen, sound-on and driven by entertainment, so ads that look like traditional commercials get skipped in seconds. The businesses that win with TikTok advertising make ads that feel like the content people came to watch: real people, quick hooks, honest demonstrations and trends adapted to their product or service.

That's why our work as a TikTok ads agency starts with creative, not settings. We study what's already performing in your category, turn those insights into concepts your team or creators can film, and test many variations quickly to find the few that break through. Then we connect the TikTok Pixel and Events API so the platform optimizes toward customers, not just views. For many businesses, TikTok becomes a way to reach new audiences that search and other social platforms miss.

Our team works in English and Spanish, with operations in the United States and Colombia. That means we can run TikTok ads for the general US market, for Spanish-speaking customers in the US, or for both from the same account, with creative, targeting and reporting in the language your customers and your team actually use.

Frequently asked questions

How much do TikTok ads cost?

TikTok advertising cost has two parts. Your ad spend is paid directly to TikTok, and you control it with daily or lifetime budgets (TikTok sets minimum budgets per campaign and ad group). Our TikTok ads management is a flat monthly fee that covers strategy, creative direction, tracking, optimization and reporting. In a free call we'll quote the management fee and recommend a starting ad budget based on your goals.

What do your TikTok marketing services include?

TikTok campaign setup and targeting, creative direction for native-style videos, Spark Ads and lead generation campaigns, TikTok Pixel and Events API tracking, ongoing testing and optimization, and a monthly results report. For e-commerce brands we also support TikTok Shop.

Do you run TikTok ads in Spanish?

Yes. RevUp is a bilingual team, so we can plan, script and target TikTok ads in English, in Spanish for Hispanic customers in the US, or both. Reports and calls can be in either language too.

Are TikTok ads worth it for a small business?

They can be, if your customers are on TikTok and your product or service can be shown in a short video: a transformation, a demo or a behind-the-scenes look. Authentic creative and a clear offer matter more than production budget. We'll tell you honestly in your free call whether TikTok should be a priority for you right now or whether another channel should come first.

Can you advertise a local business on TikTok?

Yes. TikTok ads for a local business can be targeted by state, city or area, so contractors, home service companies, clinics and med spas reach people inside their service area. We pair local targeting with lead forms or a landing page so you can see which ads bring in real inquiries.

What do I need to start running TikTok ads?

A TikTok for Business account (free to create), a TikTok Ads Manager account with a payment method, a website or lead form to send people to, and vertical videos that follow TikTok's ad specs and policies. We handle the account setup, business verification, TikTok Pixel installation and creative planning so you can launch without guesswork.

TikTok Ads Manager vs. Promote: what's the difference?

Promote boosts a single post from the TikTok app with basic targeting and limited tracking. TikTok Ads Manager gives you full campaigns: audience targeting, conversion tracking with the Pixel, creative testing and lead or sales objectives. Promote is fine for a quick boost; Ads Manager is where we build campaigns designed to bring in leads and sales.

Do I need to film content myself?

Not necessarily. We plan the concepts and can work with your team, creators or existing footage. Authentic, simple content often outperforms polished productions on TikTok.

What are Spark Ads?

Spark Ads let you promote organic TikTok posts, yours or a creator's, as ads, keeping the likes, comments and shares that make them more credible.

How do I choose the best TikTok marketing agency?

Start with how the agency approaches creative, because on TikTok the video does much of the targeting work. Then ask how they track leads and sales, whether you keep ownership of your ad account and data, and what reporting you'll get. As a TikTok advertising agency, RevUp works inside your own TikTok Ads Manager account, measures results with the TikTok Pixel and Events API, and reports on cost per result and return, not just views.

How soon will I see results from TikTok ads?

Paid ads can bring leads and sales in the first weeks once tracking is in place. The first stretch is a testing period: we launch several creative concepts, keep the ones with the lowest cost per lead or sale and replace the rest. We report on results every month.

Do I keep ownership of my TikTok ad account?

Yes. We work inside your own TikTok Ads Manager account, so the account, the Pixel data and the audiences stay yours. Ad spend is paid directly to TikTok from your payment method.

TikTok Ads or Facebook Ads: which is better for my business?

It depends on where your customers are and what kind of content you can produce. Facebook and Instagram offer lead forms and click-to-WhatsApp ads that work well for local businesses; TikTok stands out when your product or service shines on video and you want to reach new audiences. Many businesses use both, and our All-Platform Ads plan manages Google, Meta and TikTok under one strategy and one monthly fee.
