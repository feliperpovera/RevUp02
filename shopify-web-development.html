Why it works

Your store is where your marketing budget either pays off or disappears. A slow product page, a confusing menu or a checkout with too many steps can undo the work of even the best ad campaign. Most shoppers arrive on their phone, decide in seconds whether to stay and leave the moment something feels hard — and a good-looking theme alone doesn't fix that.

That's why we treat Shopify website design as a sales problem, not just a visual one. As a Shopify development agency that also runs paid advertising, we design product pages, collections and landing pages around how ad traffic actually behaves, keep every page fast on mobile and install tracking from day one, so you can see which campaigns and products drive sales. Your store becomes a tool that grows with your marketing, not a template that sits still.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to set up a Shopify store?

It depends on scope: how many products and pages you have, whether a customized theme is enough or you need custom development, and whether we're migrating an existing site. We quote each Shopify project after a free call, so you know the cost before work starts. Your Shopify subscription and any paid apps or themes are billed directly to you by Shopify and the app developers.

How much is a Shopify website per month?

Shopify charges a monthly subscription based on the plan you choose, plus any paid apps you add. Those are paid directly to Shopify and app developers, not to us. If you want our team to keep maintaining and improving the store after launch, ongoing store management is an optional service we scope with you.

What does a Shopify development agency do?

A Shopify development agency plans, designs, builds and improves Shopify stores. With RevUp that covers store design and setup, theme customization, custom features and app integrations, migrations, technical SEO, tracking and ongoing optimization — plus Google, Meta and TikTok ads to bring shoppers to the store if you want them.

Should I hire a Shopify expert or a freelance Shopify developer?

A freelance Shopify developer can be a good fit for a small, well-defined fix. When design, development, SEO, tracking and marketing need to work together, a team of Shopify experts keeps everything consistent and accountable. If your project doesn't need an agency, we'll tell you on the free call.

Is Shopify a good website builder for my business?

If you sell products online, usually yes: Shopify is reliable, secure, easy to manage and has a large app ecosystem. If you run a service business that mainly needs calls, quotes or bookings, a standard website is often a better fit, and our website design service covers that. We'll recommend the right platform for your catalog and operations.

Can you redesign my Shopify store or migrate my site to Shopify?

Yes. We redesign existing Shopify stores and migrate stores from other platforms, carrying over your products and content and setting up redirects to protect the search traffic you've already built.

Will my Shopify store be optimized for Google?

Yes. Every store we build includes technical SEO basics: fast load times, mobile-friendly design, clean URLs, proper titles and descriptions, image alt text, a sitemap and product structured data. That gives Google a clear picture of your store; rankings then depend on your content, your competition and time.
