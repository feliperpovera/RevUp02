Facebook & Instagram ads agency: campaigns that sell, not just scroll

RevUp is a Facebook ads agency for small businesses, contractors, home service companies and e-commerce brands across the United States. Your customers are on Facebook and Instagram every day — we plan, build and manage the ads that stop the scroll, reach the right people in your service area and turn that attention into calls, leads and sales.

Chat on WhatsApp

Why it works

On Facebook and Instagram, people aren't searching for you — you're interrupting their feed. That means the creative does most of the work. The same offer can succeed or fail depending on the first three seconds of a video, the headline or whether the ad looks like an ad at all. Meanwhile, privacy changes have made accurate tracking harder, so accounts without a proper Conversions API setup often optimize on incomplete data.

As a Facebook ads agency, we tackle both sides. On the creative side, we test hooks, formats and offers in a structured way instead of guessing, and we keep a steady flow of new ads so results don't fade as audiences see the same creative repeatedly. On the data side, we set up the Meta Pixel and Conversions API together so the algorithm learns from real purchases and leads. Better data plus better creative is what lets a Meta Ads account scale profitably.

Frequently asked questions

What is Meta advertising?

+

Meta advertising is paid advertising across Meta's apps — Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp, plus the Audience Network — managed from one Meta Ads Manager account. Meta is the company behind Facebook and Instagram, so Meta ads and Facebook ads refer to the same platform.

Are Facebook ads worth it for small business?

+

They can be, when the offer is clear, the targeting matches your service area and tracking is set up correctly. Facebook ads for small business reach people before they search, so they work best with a strong offer and fast lead follow-up. In your free call we'll tell you honestly whether Meta ads fit your business or whether another channel should come first.

Do Facebook ads work for contractors and home service businesses?

+

They can. Roofers, remodelers, painters and HVAC, cleaning and landscaping companies use Facebook and Instagram to reach homeowners in their area with offers like free estimates or inspections, collect leads through instant forms or Messenger and retarget people who visited their website. What makes Facebook ads for home services work is tight local targeting, real project photos and video, and calling leads back quickly.

How much does Facebook ads management cost?

+

Facebook ads management has two costs: a flat monthly management fee to RevUp, and your ad spend, which you pay directly to Meta and is not included in our fee. You can compare our Meta Ads and all-platform plans in the Plans & pricing section on this page.

How much should a small business spend on Facebook ads?

+

You can start with a modest budget, but you need enough spend for the algorithm to learn who is most likely to become a customer. We'll recommend a starting Facebook and Instagram ads budget in your free call based on your goals, your service area and what a lead or sale is worth to you, then scale what works.

Should I advertise on Facebook or Instagram?

+

Usually both. Meta ads run across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and the Audience Network from one account, and the algorithm shifts budget to where your results are cheapest. If you're looking for an Instagram ads agency specifically, we also run Instagram-first campaigns with Reels and Stories creative built for the format.

Do you create the ads?

+

Yes. We plan and produce ad creative — copy, images and video concepts — and test them systematically to find what performs.

What results have your clients seen?

+

Results vary by business, but RevUp clients have reached returns on ad spend such as 20.2x for MIAN and 7.4x for New Life Furniture across their paid advertising.
