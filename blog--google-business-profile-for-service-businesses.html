Google Business Profile: how local service businesses win on Google Maps

Categories, service areas, reviews and the everyday habits that help plumbers, clinics, contractors and other local service businesses appear in Google Maps and the map pack.

Why your Google Business Profile matters so much

When someone searches for a local service — "dentist near me", "roof repair", "personal injury lawyer" — Google often shows a map with a few businesses before the regular results. Those businesses get calls, direction requests and website visits without paying for a click. Your Google Business Profile is what decides whether you're in that group.

It's free, and for many local service businesses it's the single most important piece of their online presence. Here's how to make the most of it.

Set it up correctly from the start

If you go to your customers instead of having them come to you — plumbers, cleaners, landscapers, mobile services — set up your profile as a service-area business. You can hide your street address and list the cities or areas you serve instead.

Use your real business name exactly as customers see it. Adding keywords or city names to your business name to rank better goes against Google's guidelines and can get your profile suspended.

Choose the right categories

Your primary category is one of the strongest signals Google uses to decide which searches you appear for. Choose the most specific category that describes your main service — for example, a more specific trade category rather than a generic one when it fits. Then add secondary categories for other services you genuinely offer.

Fill in every detail

Complete profiles give Google and customers more reasons to choose you. Make sure you have:

  • Services listed individually, with short descriptions
  • Accurate hours, including holiday hours
  • A phone number that's answered, and your website link
  • A description that explains what you do and where, in plain language
  • Attributes that apply to you, such as language spoken or online appointments

Add real photos regularly

Photos of your team, vehicles, office and finished work show that you're a real, active business. Skip stock images. Add new photos over time — completed projects are perfect content for service businesses.

Build a steady flow of reviews

Reviews influence both your ranking and whether people call you. The most reliable way to get them is simply to ask every customer, right after the job, with a direct link to your review form. Reply to every review — thank people for positive ones and respond calmly and helpfully to negative ones.

Never buy reviews, write them yourself or offer rewards in exchange for positive ratings. It violates Google's policies and can lead to reviews being removed or your profile being restricted.

Keep your information consistent everywhere

Your business name, phone number and address or service area should match across your website, your profile, social media and directories like Yelp, the BBB and industry sites. Consistent information helps Google trust that your business details are accurate.

Post updates and measure results

Use profile updates to share offers, seasonal services and recent projects. In your profile's performance section you can see how many people called, requested directions or clicked to your website. Add tracking parameters to your website link so visits from your profile show up clearly in Google Analytics.

If you also run Google Ads, link your profile to your ads account so your address and map information can appear in your ads through location assets.

Common mistakes

Watch out for these:

  • Keyword-stuffed business names
  • Using a virtual office or mailbox as a physical address
  • Leaving categories, services or hours incomplete
  • Ignoring reviews or only replying to good ones
  • Letting the profile go months without updates or new photos

Next steps
