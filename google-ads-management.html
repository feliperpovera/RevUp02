Why it works

Google Ads is different from every other channel because it reaches people at the exact moment they are looking for a solution. Someone searching for "emergency plumber", "AC repair" or "leather jackets for men" is already close to buying. That's why Google Ads for HVAC contractors, plumbers, roofing companies and other home services — and for e-commerce brands — can be such a direct way to win customers. The catch is that it's also easy to waste money: broad keywords, missing negative keywords, ads that send people to the wrong page and tracking that counts the wrong actions can quietly drain a budget.

Good Google Ads management is about closing those leaks. We build around keywords with real purchase intent, add negative keywords every week based on actual search terms, match every ad to a relevant landing page and make sure Google is optimizing for revenue and qualified leads instead of cheap clicks. The result is an account where you can see exactly what each dollar returns — and decide with confidence when to invest more.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Google Ads management cost?

+

There are two separate costs. Your ad spend is paid directly to Google, so you control it and see every charge. Our PPC management fee is a flat monthly fee that covers strategy, campaign builds, tracking, weekly optimization and reporting. You can compare the plans in the Plans & pricing section on this page; packages that add SEO include a one-time SEO launch.

How much should a small business spend on Google Ads?

+

It depends on your industry, competition, service area and what a new customer is worth to you. On a free call we look at your market and help you set a starting budget you're comfortable testing, then scale only when the numbers support it. You can raise or lower your ad spend at any time.

Is Google Ads worth it for a small business?

+

It can be, when the account is built around searches with real buying intent and tracked properly. Google Ads reaches people at the moment they're looking for your service, but broad keywords and missing tracking can waste budget fast. We start with your most profitable service, measure every call and form, and tell you honestly if the numbers don't work for your business.

Is a Google Ads specialist worth it, or should I hire an agency?

+

A specialist is worth it when your time is better spent running your business than learning the platform. A freelancer can handle a simple account; a PPC agency like RevUp gives you a team for strategy, ad copy, landing page recommendations, conversion tracking and reporting. We work with businesses across the United States remotely, by video call and WhatsApp.

What are Google Local Services Ads, and do you manage them?

+

Local Services Ads are Google ads for service businesses such as plumbers, HVAC companies, roofers, cleaners and law firms. They can appear at the top of search results with your reviews and a Google verification badge, and you're charged per lead instead of per click. Eligibility depends on your category and location, and Google runs its own verification. Google Local Services Ads management is part of our Google Ads plan: we guide you through setup and verification, manage budgets and review leads alongside your Search campaigns.

Do Google Ads work for contractors?

+

They can, because they put you in front of people actively searching for the jobs you do — AC repair, a roof replacement, a plumbing emergency. Results depend on your market, budget, offer and how fast you answer the phone. We build Google Ads for contractors and home services around your most profitable services and service area, track every call and cut the keywords that bring bad leads.

How long does it take for Google Ads to work?

+

Search campaigns can start bringing clicks and calls within days of launch. Most accounts need a few weeks of conversion data before automated bidding settles and your cost per lead or sale becomes stable, and we optimize every week along the way.

Can you take over my existing Google Ads account? Do I keep ownership?

+

Yes to both. We start with an audit of your current campaigns, tracking and spend, then fix what's wasting budget before we scale. Your Google Ads account, data and assets always belong to you — we work inside it with manager access.
